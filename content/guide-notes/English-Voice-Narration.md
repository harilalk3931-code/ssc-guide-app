1. **Convert to Passive Voice: 'The mechanic repaired the car yesterday.' [SSC CGL 2023 Tier-I (15-Jul-Shift-1)] [Set 1]**
   - A) The car was repaired by the mechanic yesterday.
   - B) The car is repaired by the mechanic yesterday.
   - C) The car has been repaired by the mechanic.
   - D) The car had been repaired by the mechanic.
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Simple Past active ('repaired') converts to 'was + V3' ('was repaired').
Why others are wrong: Option B changes tense to Simple Present; Option C changes tense to Present Perfect; Option D changes tense to Past Perfect.

2. **Convert to Indirect Speech: 'He said to me, "Where are you going for your holidays?"' [SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I (10-Sep-Shift-2)] [Set 2]**
   - A) He asked me where I was going for my holidays.
   - B) He asked me where was I going for my holidays.
   - C) He told me where I am going for my holidays.
   - D) He inquired me where I had been going for my holidays.
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Reporting verb 'said to' changes to 'asked'. Indirect interrogative converts question word order to assertive order (Subject 'I' before Verb 'was going'). Tense backshifts from Present Continuous to Past Continuous.
Why others are wrong: Option B retains inverted question word order; Option C fails to backshift tense; Option D changes tense to Past Perfect Continuous.
