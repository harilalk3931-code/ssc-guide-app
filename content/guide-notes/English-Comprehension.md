1. **Rearrange the parts of the sentence in correct order:
We have been
P. able to identify certain patterns in
Q. the climate data collected over
R. the past three decades
S. through satellite imaging [SSC CGL 2023 Tier-I (1-Dec-5.15PM-EN)] [Set 1]**
   - A) PQRS
   - B) QPSR
   - C) RSPQ
   - D) PRSQ
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Sentence starts with subject-verb 'We have been'. Part P ('able to identify certain patterns in') completes the predicate adjective phrase. Part Q ('the climate data collected over') provides the object. Part R ('the past three decades') provides time duration. Part S ('through satellite imaging') provides method. Logical sequence: P-Q-R-S.
Why others are wrong: Option B (QPSR) starts with noun fragment 'the climate data' breaking main verb 'have been'; Option C (RSPQ) places prepositional time phrase first awkwardly; Option D (PRSQ) separates time phrase from its preceding object.

2. **Given below are four jumbled sentences. Select the option that gives their correct order:
A. He then moved to London for higher studies.
B. Jawaharlal Nehru was born in Prayagraj in 1889.
C. There he studied law and qualified as a barrister.
D. He returned to India in 1912 to join the freedom movement. [SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I (12-Sep-Shift-1)] [Set 2]**
   - A) B-A-C-D
   - B) A-B-C-D
   - C) B-C-A-D
   - D) D-A-C-B
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Sentence B introduces the proper noun subject ('Jawaharlal Nehru was born...'). Sentence A follows with chronological travel ('He then moved to London...'). Sentence C expands on his stay in London ('There he studied law...'). Sentence D concludes with his return to India in 1912. Correct sequence: B-A-C-D.
Why others are wrong: Option B (A-B-C-D) starts with pronoun 'He'; Option C (B-C-A-D) places 'There (London)' before mentioning London; Option D (D-A-C-B) starts with return to India.
