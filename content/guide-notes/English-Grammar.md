### Quick Practice (150 MCQs)

1. **Identify the segment containing a grammatical error: 'Neither the manager nor his assistants was present at the annual meeting.' [SSC CGL 2023 Tier-I (12-Dec-Shift-1)] [Variation 1]**
   - A) Neither the manager
   - B) nor his assistants
   - C) was present at
   - D) the annual meeting
   - **Answer: C**
   - Explanation: When subjects are joined by 'neither...nor', the verb agrees with the closer subject ('his assistants', plural). It must be 'were present'.
Why others are wrong: Option A is the first subject frame; Option B correctly pairs 'nor' with plural 'assistants'; Option D is a prepositional phrase.

2. **Identify the segment containing a grammatical error: 'The number of applicants seeking admission have increased rapidly this year.' [SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I (13-Sep-Shift-2)] [Variation 2]**
   - A) The number of applicants
   - B) seeking admission
   - C) have increased rapidly
   - D) this year
   - **Answer: C**
   - Explanation: 'The number of' takes a singular verb ('has increased'), whereas 'A number of' takes a plural verb.
Why others are wrong: Option A correctly uses 'The number of'; Option B is a participial modifier; Option D is a time adverbial.

3. **Spot the error: 'Hardly had he entered the station than the train left.' [SSC CGL 2023 Tier-I (14-Jul-Shift-3)] [Variation 3]**
   - A) Hardly had he
   - B) entered the station
   - C) than the train
   - D) left
   - **Answer: C**
   - Explanation: 'Hardly' and 'Scarcely' take 'when' or 'before' as correlative conjunctions, not 'than'. 'No sooner' takes 'than'.
Why others are wrong: Option A correctly applies inversion 'had he'; Option B is past participle phrase; Option D is main verb clause.
