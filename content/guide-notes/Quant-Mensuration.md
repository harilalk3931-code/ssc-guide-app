   - A) 113.14 cm^3
   - B) 226.28 cm^3
   - C) 56.57 cm^3
   - D) 339.42 cm^3
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Volume of Sphere V = (4/3) * pi * r^3 = (4/3) * (22/7) * (3^3) = (4/3) * (22/7) * 27 = 36 * 3.1428 = 113.14 cm^3.
