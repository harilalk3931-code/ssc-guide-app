1. **A dishonest dealer sells goods at cost price but uses a weight of 900g instead of 1 kg. Find his profit percentage. [SSC CGL 2024 Tier-I (14-Sep-Shift-1)] [Q1]**
   - A) 11.11%
   - B) 10.00%
   - C) 12.50%
   - D) 9.09%
   - **Answer: A**
   - Explanation: Profit % = (Error / True Weight - Error) * 100 = (100g / 900g) * 100 = 100/9 = 11.11%.
Why others are wrong: Option B divides error by 1000g; Option C uses 800g weight; Option D divides error by 1100g.
